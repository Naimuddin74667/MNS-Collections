Amazon fees used and how returns are counted
  • Referral fee, Fashion Jewellery: 0% up to ₹1,000; 22.5% above ₹1,000.
  • Easy Ship closing fee (from 7 Sep 2026): ₹2 up to ₹300 · ₹23 for ₹301–500 · ₹48 for ₹501–1,000 · ₹79 above.
  • Self Ship closing fee: ₹20 · ₹26 · ₹51 · ₹101 for the same bands.
  • Easy Ship shipping (flat, all India, from 16 Mar 2026), Standard: ₹55 first 500 g · ₹75 up to 1 kg · ₹112 up to 2 kg · +₹34/kg to 5 kg · +₹18/kg after. Basic ₹4 more, Premium ₹2 less.
  • Amazon adds 18% GST on all its fees. Unless you tick the credit box, it's counted as a cost.
  • Each return costs the forward fees again, a reverse shipment (assumed equal to forward shipping), a new box, and the product cost for pieces you can't resell.
  • TCS (0.5%) and TDS (0.1%) are held from payouts but can be claimed back, so they aren't counted as costs.
  • Rates read from sell.amazon.in on 27 Nov 2026. Confirm with Seller Central's fee preview once a listing is live.
